01Day by day

The plan — stage by stage.

Distances and difficulty adapt to the group on the day. This is the shape of the tour, not a stopwatch.

  1. Day 0

    Arrival day

    Transfer to Ouarzazate, meet the group, dinner and route briefing.

  2. Day 1

    Ouarzazate to Nkob

    Into the Anti-Atlas: Berber villages, mountain pistes and the Draa Valley.

  3. Day 2

    Nkob to Sidi Ali

    Old caravan trails, open plains and riverbeds. Night in a riad.

  4. Day 3

    Sidi Ali to Erg Chegaga

    Sand and hamada building up to the big dunes. Night at a desert camp.

  5. Day 4

    Erg Chegaga to Foum Zguid

    Dunes, the old Paris–Dakar piste, Lac Iriki and the salt flats.

  6. Day 5

    Foum Zguid to Ouarzazate

    Mountains and valleys back to base. Farewell dinner.

  7. Day 6

    Departure day

    Transfer and flights home.
