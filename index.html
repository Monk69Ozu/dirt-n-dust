06The bikes

KTM 450 EXC-F & GasGas EC 450F.

One standardised, well-maintained fleet. 450cc singles with controllable power and the torque soft ground demands — light chassis, quality suspension, at home in dunes and on rocky trails.

  • 2023/2024 machines, serviced between every tour
  • Mousses and fresh enduro tyres — no puncture roulette
  • Accessible for newer riders, capable for experienced ones
  • Lowered bikes, bar risers and stiffer suspension on request
  • Fuel and third-party insurance included

The fleet rides with guide and support crew — unsupported rental only by special arrangement.

08Need to know

The practical stuff.

Licence, flights, damage deposit, gear — the questions every rider asks before booking.

Do I need a motorcycle licence?+

Yes — a full motorcycle licence, your passport and suitable travel/medical insurance are required on every tour.

How do I get there?+

Direct flights to Ouarzazate run from Paris, Marseille and Barcelona. Marrakech has direct connections from Gatwick, Luton, Stansted, Manchester, Liverpool, Edinburgh, Bristol and Newcastle.

What happens if I damage the bike?+

Scratches and scuffs are part of riding and tolerated. Broken parts are charged at parts price. A damage deposit of €600 / £500 applies.

How far do we ride each day?+

Most days cover 120–250 km with around 5–7 hours on the bike, including breaks. Pace and difficulty adapt to the group.

Can I rent riding gear?+

Yes — helmet, boots and full body armour can be rented per day (full set €30/day, single items €10/day).
